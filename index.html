What We Fix

Our Services

No job too small. No pair too far gone. We've seen it all and fixed most of it.

01

Resoling

Full and half sole replacements in leather, rubber, or crepe. Your favorite shoes don't have to be retired just because the bottom is worn through.

02

Heel Replacement

Worn heels replaced on dress shoes, boots, and heels — men's and women's. We keep the look right and the alignment true.

03

Shoe Repair

About the Shop

A Neighborhood Institution

Menlo Joe's has been fixing shoes on El Camino Real for decades. No frills, no gimmicks — just a skilled cobbler who knows his craft and takes pride in every pair that comes through the door.

We work on everything: dress shoes, work boots, sneakers, heels, sandals. If it goes on a foot and it's broken, there's a good chance we can fix it. And if we can't, we'll tell you that too — we don't take jobs we can't do well.

The Peninsula has trusted us for years because we do what we say and say what we do. Honest prices. Good turnaround. Quality that lasts.
