Our Story

Old-World Craft,
Modern Precision

Menlo Joe's Shoe Repair has been the go-to cobbler on the Peninsula for decades — a neighborhood institution built on honest work and genuine pride in the craft.

We believe great shoes are worth saving. Whether it's your favorite leather dress shoes, everyday boots, or a cherished pair of heels, our skilled cobblers use traditional techniques and the finest materials to restore them to their full glory — often better than new.

Located right on El Camino Real in the heart of Menlo Park, we're easy to find and even easier to trust. Drop in, say hello, and let us take care of the rest.
