About the Shop

A Neighborhood Institution

Old Town Cobbler has been fixing shoes on Main Street for decades. No frills, no gimmicks — just a skilled cobbler who knows his craft and takes pride in every pair that comes through the door.

We work on everything: dress shoes, work boots, sneakers, heels, sandals. If it goes on a foot and it's broken, there's a good chance we can fix it. And if we can't, we'll tell you that too — we don't take jobs we can't do well.

This town has trusted us for years because we do what we say and say what we do. Honest prices. Good turnaround. Quality that lasts.
